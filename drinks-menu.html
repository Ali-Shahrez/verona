Cocktails

Signature & Classic

  • Pornstar Martini£11.95

    Vanilla vodka, Passoã passion fruit liqueur, pineapple juice and vanilla sugar syrup. Served with a shot of prosecco on the side — stylish, fun and evocative.

  • Espresso Martini£11.95

    Vodka, coffee liqueur, espresso and simple syrup, shaken well with ice and garnished with three coffee beans. A great little pick-me-up.

  • Cosmopolitan£11.95

    Vodka, Cointreau, cranberry juice and lime juice. Sweet, tangy and delicious — cheers!

  • Margarita£11.95

    Tequila, lime juice and orange liqueur with a salt rim garnish — a famously sweet, sour and salty cocktail.

  • Negroni£12.95

    Sweet vermouth, Campari and gin. A timeless classic known for its bittersweet flavour — great before dinner or as a nightcap.

  • Amaretto Sour£12.95

    Amaretto, lemon juice and sugar syrup with a sweet-sharp almond flavour, garnished with a cocktail cherry. Like Bakewell tart in a glass.

  • Aperol Spritz£8.95

    The citrus, herbal flavours of Aperol with the fizz of Prosecco and soda — a refreshing drink to enjoy all year round.

  • Limoncello Spritz£8.95

    Limoncello, prosecco and soda — a refreshing combination that pairs beautifully with light meals.

  • Hugo Spritz£9.95

    Prosecco, soda water and St. Germain elderflower liqueur, garnished with mint — a lighter, sweeter alternative to the Aperol Spritz.
