Chef de Partie

Kitchen 4–5 Years' Experience
Key Responsibilities
  • Overseeing a designated section, including food preparation, cooking, and presentation to high standards.
  • Maintaining food hygiene standards and minimising waste.
  • Supporting the sous chef and coordinating with other stations.

02

Pot Washer

Kitchen Weekends Required
Key Responsibilities
  • Washing up kitchen equipment, trays, bowls, and utensils — mainly using a sink system and dishwasher, around 90% manual washing.
  • Keeping the washing-up area clean and organised throughout your shift.
  • Supporting the team to maintain a hygienic and safe working environment.
Requirements
  • A positive, can-do attitude and willingness to get stuck in.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
  • Good physical stamina — this is a physical role that involves standing and scrubbing for long periods.
  • Punctuality and reliability.
  • Must be flexible and able to commit to working weekends.
